## Who we are

WiserWits ("we", "us") operates the WiserWits student mobile app. This policy
explains what personal data the app processes, why, and your choices. For
questions, contact **support@wiserwits.com**.

## What we collect

**Account & profile.** Your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
For students enrolled through a school ("partner"), your school, class/section
and enrollment are associated with your account.

**Academic & wellness data.** Attendance, marks, exam results, report cards,
timetable, assignments, holistic assessments, teacher feedback, and advice
threads — provided by your school or entered by you or an authorized contributor.
Health & wellness data you or a contributor add (BMI height/weight readings,
doctor consultations, diet plans, lab reports). Health data is sensitive; we
process it only to show it back to you and the people you authorize.

**Device & notification data.** When you enable notifications, we store an Expo
push token and a device label (your device name/model and platform) so we can
deliver notifications to your devices. You can remove a device at any time in
**Account & Security**.

**Payment data.** Subscriptions and paid courses are processed by **Razorpay**.
Payment card details are entered into Razorpay's secure checkout and are **not
collected or stored by WiserWits**. We store the resulting order/payment
references and your plan/course entitlements.

**Support communications.** If you email support, we process the contents of
your message to help you.

We do **not** sell your personal data.

## How we use your data

- To provide the app: authenticate you, show your academic, wellness and
  learning data, and process subscriptions and course purchases.
- To send notifications you've opted into (new marks, attendance, report cards,
  assignments, advice/feedback, and health updates). You control these per
  category in **Settings**, and can turn them off entirely.
- To keep the service secure and working (e.g. session refresh, fraud/abuse
  prevention on payments).

## Multiple accounts

The app supports adding several accounts on one device (e.g. a parent managing
more than one child). Each account's data is kept separate and each is
independently signed out.

## Sharing

- **Your school/partner** and the **contributors you authorize** can view or
  fill the specific data you grant them (see Contributors). You can revoke a
  contributor's access at any time.
- **Service providers** who process data on our behalf: our hosting/database
  provider, **Razorpay** (payments), and **Expo** (push notification delivery).
- **Legal**: where required by law.

## Retention

We keep your account and associated data while your account is active. Push
tokens are removed when you sign out of a device, remove a device, or when the
push provider reports the token is no longer valid.

## Your choices & rights

- **Notifications**: enable/disable overall or per category in Settings; manage
  devices in Account & Security.
- **Password**: change it in Account & Security.
- **Access / deletion**: to request access to or deletion of your account and
  data, email **support@wiserwits.com**. (In-app self-serve deletion is planned;
  until then, deletion requests are handled by support.)

## Children's privacy

WiserWits is used by students, including minors, typically through a school or a
parent/guardian. Schools and guardians are responsible for the accounts they
create and manage. We process student data to provide the educational service.

## Changes

We may update this policy; we'll post the new version at the policy URL and
update the "Last updated" date.
